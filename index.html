About

Star Computer Institute

Star Computer Institute at Station Square, Bhubaneswar helps students, job seekers, and working professionals build practical computer skills. Our focus is simple: clear teaching, regular practice, and courses that match what offices and businesses actually use.

Whether you are starting from basics or upgrading to Tally, Advanced Excel, graphic design, or web design, we keep batches small enough for personal attention and progress you can see.

Who we train

  • Beginners with little or no computer background
  • Students preparing for office and accounting roles
  • Working professionals who need Excel and GST skills
  • Learners interested in design and web basics
